Who We Are

S&B Fresh & Crispy is a modern fast-food brand created with one simple vision: To serve fresh, crispy and delicious meals made with premium ingredients.

Every burger, every wing and every nugget is prepared with care so our customers enjoy the perfect taste in every bite.

Founder & CEO

Muhammad Waseem

Muhammad Waseem is the Founder and CEO of S&B Fresh & Crispy. With a passion for entrepreneurship and innovation, he established S&B with the dream of creating a trusted Pakistani fast-food brand known for quality, freshness and customer satisfaction.

His vision is to expand S&B across Pakistan while maintaining world-class food standards and exceptional customer service.
